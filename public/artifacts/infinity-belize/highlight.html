How to Present This Engagement
Step 01 — Open With

The Deal Structure Diagram

Start by showing the 12-entity capital stack diagram. Ask the prospect to count the entities. Then say: we designed the relationships between all of them. Let the complexity land before you explain any of it.

Step 02 — Anchor With

The Economic Impact Table

Open the investor deck to the hospitality revenue model: 4,860 keys, three star ratings, $485M gross revenue, $2.7B national impact. This shows the financial model was built on real inputs, not narrative assumptions.

Step 03 — Close With

The Government Photos

End with the photo evidence: City Council chambers, Investment Summit podium, Prime Minister meeting. These prove the work moved from document to room — from strategy to sovereign-level credibility.
