Five independent revenue streams — all modeled separately
Hardware sales, upgrade customizations, technology add-ons, maintenance services, and SaaS subscriptions — each with its own cost structure, growth rate, and margin profile. SaaS grows from near-zero to 60% of gross revenue by FY25, at zero additional production cost.
✓
Survived outside accountant review verified
An interested investor retained outside accountants to scrutinize the pro forma. The model required no material revision. That same investor then made an unsolicited job offer to the consultant responsible for building it.
$
Valuation and equity structure embedded in the model
Current company value of $12M derived from 5× FY22 EBITDA — the standard multiple for a product-asset business with $1M+ annual gross. Initial $250K angel investment priced at 5% equity based on a $5M valuation, representing a 59.3% discount to the modeled current value. The discount structure created investor urgency without desperation.
↻
Business model redesign: hardware to hardware + SaaS highest impact
The client entered the engagement as a hardware company. The consultant identified that the Defender's smart-lock and access-monitoring features were not just product features — they were the foundation of a recurring revenue business. The SaaS pivot restructured the entire financial model and substantially increased the company's long-term valuation multiple. By Year 3, SaaS was projected to represent 35% of gross revenue at zero additional production cost.
◎
Go-to-market repositioning built on two non-obvious levers
Rather than conventional marketing spend, the strategy used (1) pursuit of a UL burglary rating to reframe the product from operational equipment to security imperative, and (2) pursuit of a parking insurance underwriter endorsement — an unprecedented move designed to create industry-level validation that no marketing budget can buy. This approach reset the market's core value propositions before a single ad ran.

Market analysis grounded in primary purchased research
The market section was built from a purchased 2020 AnythingResearch Premium Report on Parking Lots & Garages registered to this engagement, NPA emergency relief press data, and AHA hospital statistics to quantify the healthcare vertical. This distinguishes the document from generic investor materials built on assumptions.

For a prospect who needs financial modeling
Lead with the assumptions tab of the pro forma — not the output statements. Show how changing a single input (SaaS attach rate, units per customer) cascades through five years of projections. That one demonstration is worth more than any explanation. Follow with the investor offer page to show the valuation methodology.
For a prospect who needs strategic advisory
Tell the SaaS pivot story. The client came in as a hardware company and left as a hardware + SaaS company. Show the difference in the FY25 revenue line with and without the SaaS layer. The number tells the story — no slides needed.
For a prospect who needs investor materials produced
Show the signed SOW alongside the executive summary. The contract demonstrates scope management and professionalism; the document demonstrates execution quality. Then land on the outcome: $500K raised in half the projected time. Let the contrast do the work.
What to always lead with, regardless of audience
"$500K raised — double the goal, half the time." That line is the first thing any prospect hears. Everything else is evidence for how it happened.
